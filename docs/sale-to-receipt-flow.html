From pump sale to ZRA receipt

How a sale at an edge-gateway station is stored, signed by ZRA, printed at the pump island and synced to the cloud.

Receipt states

pending→ ZRA signs → signed→ printer prints → printed  or, if ZRA rejects the sale,failed

Step by step

  1. The sale reaches the gateway. When the nozzle is hung up, the PTS-2 sends an UploadPumpTransaction (jsonPTS, HMAC-signed) to the gateway's listener.
  2. The gateway stores it safely. In one local database transaction it saves the sale, a fiscal_receipts row with status pending, and outbox entries. Only after that commit does it reply OK, and the PTS-2 then clears the sale from its SD card. From here on, the sale cannot be lost.
  3. The invoice number is fixed up front. It is deterministic (branch plus a running series), so a retry after a timeout cannot create a second invoice or a double signature; ZRA's saveSales is idempotent on that number.
  4. ZRA signs it. The fiscal pipeline claims the pending receipt (FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED with a lease) and sends saveSales to the ZRA VSDC. ZRA returns the receipt number, total receipt number, internal data, signature, SDC id, MRC number and QR URL. The receipt becomes signed.
  5. The receipt prints. The gateway prints it at the pump island: sale lines, VAT breakdown, ZRA receipt number and signature, and the QR code customers can scan to verify. The receipt becomes printed, and staff can reprint it from the gateway.
  6. The cloud gets every step. Each status change goes into the outbox in the same transaction as the change, and the sync loop sends it to cloud /ingest. That is how the portal shows the ZRA receipt number for each sale.

When something fails

ProblemWhat happens
ZRA unreachable or slowThe receipt stays pending and retries with exponential backoff, from 2 s up to 5 min, while sales keep flowing. If it is still unsigned after 60 minutes, the cloud fiscal-worker signs it instead. Only one path can sign a given invoice.
ZRA rejects the sale (non-retryable)The receipt is marked failed and an alert is raised. An operator can re-queue it once the problem is fixed.
Printer jammed or out of paperThe receipt is already signed and stays that way; only printing retries, with backoff. The ZRA record never depends on paper.
Internet downSteps 1 to 5 still work if the station can reach ZRA (fully offline when the VSDC is on site; otherwise signing waits as above). Step 6 catches up when the link returns.

Direct-mode stations

Stations without a gateway skip steps 2 to 5 at the station. The PTS-2 posts straight to cloud /ingest/pts, and the cloud fiscal-worker signs the sale with ZRA about 2 minutes later. Nothing prints at the pump, so the customer gets no fiscal receipt from the FMS there. That is one of the main reasons the edge gateway is the default.
