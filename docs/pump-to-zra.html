From pump to ZRA

The two ways a sale reaches ZRA Smart Invoice (VSDC) in the FMS deployment: from the station's edge gateway, or from the cloud fiscal-worker.

Edge-gateway station

LUS-0001, COP-0001. ZRA is called from inside the station.

  1. Pump → PTS-2: the dispenser reports the sale over the forecourt pump line.
  2. PTS-2 → edge gateway (station network): an HMAC-signed jsonPTS UploadPumpTransaction to port 8100.
  3. Edge gateway → ZRA VSDC: the sale and a pending receipt are saved in the local database (edge-db), then the fiscal pipeline sends saveSales over HTTPS (ZRA_VSDC_URL; on site or over the internet).
  4. ZRA → station: receipt number, signature and QR data come back; the receipt becomes signed and prints on the station's fiscal printer.
  5. Station → cloud, afterwards: the outbox carries the sale and signed receipt to proxy → ingest-api → PostgreSQL. ZRA is not called again.

Direct-mode station

LUS-1001, SOU-0001. ZRA is called from the cloud.

  1. Pump → PTS-2: same as at an edge station.
  2. PTS-2 → cloud (internet): the jsonPTS upload goes to proxy/ingress → ingest-api at /ingest/pts.
  3. Ingest: ingest-api checks the HMAC and stores the sale in PostgreSQL with fiscal_status = pending.
  4. fiscal-worker → ZRA VSDC: about every 5 minutes the scheduler queues sales pending for at least 2 minutes as fiscalise jobs; a worker sends saveSales (ZRA_VSDC_URL, or the device for the station's TPIN and branch in ZRA_VSDC_DEVICES).
  5. ZRA → cloud: the worker saves the fiscal_receipts row and marks the sale signed. Nothing prints at the station.

Never signed twice. The invoice number is fixed when the sale is recorded, and a sale can have only one receipt, so the edge path, the direct path and the 60-minute cloud fallback cannot double-sign a sale.

Local Docker stack. "ZRA VSDC" is the mock-vsdc container at http://mock-vsdc:8200; both the edge gateway and the fiscal-worker call it in place of the real ZRA endpoint.
